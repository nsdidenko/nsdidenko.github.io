*Last updated: October 21, 2026.*

Model Score ("the app") is made by Mikita Dzidzenka ("we", "us"). The app connects you with a modeling-industry expert who reviews your photos and measurements. This policy explains what we collect, why, and how to delete it.

## Who can use the app
The app is for adults aged 18 and over. We ask you to confirm your age on first launch. If our expert has doubts that a request comes from an adult, the request is rejected and the photos are deleted.

## What we collect
When you send a request, you give us:
- **Two photos**: a portrait and a full-length photo.
- **Your name**: so the expert can address you.
- **Your parameters**: height, hips, waist, bust, city and country (typed by you), modeling experience, visible tattoos or piercings.

We also process:
- **An anonymous user ID** created by Firebase Authentication. We do not ask for your email, phone or Apple ID to use the app.
- **A device mark**: a random key that the app keeps in this iPhone's Keychain, and a record on our server saying only "the free review on this device has been used" and when. It contains no name, photo, parameters, user ID or request. We use it to keep the free review to one per iPhone and prevent abuse.
- **Purchase records** (whether you bought the full review), handled by RevenueCat and Apple. We never see your card details.
- **Usage events** (for example, "verdict viewed" or "purchase completed"), sent to Amplitude with the same anonymous ID and without your photos, name or parameters.
- **A push notification token**, used only to tell you that the expert has answered.

There is no account: sign-in is anonymous.

We do not track you across other companies' apps or websites, we do not show ads, and we do not sell your data.

## How we use it
- The expert uses your photos and parameters to write your verdict and, if you buy it, your full review.
- The anonymous ID links your request and purchase to this device.
- The device mark lets us offer one free review per iPhone, even if the app is reinstalled or your data is deleted.
- Usage events help us understand which parts of the app work and fix problems.

## Who sees your photos
Only the expert. Your photos and results are private: they are never published, ranked or compared with other users. If you choose to share your result card, you decide what goes on it; your photo is not included unless you add it.

## Where data is stored
Photos and request data are stored in Google Firebase (Cloud Storage and Firestore). The device mark is stored in Firestore. Purchase data is processed by RevenueCat and Apple. Usage events are processed by Amplitude. These providers act on our behalf.

## How long we keep it
- **Photos are deleted automatically 7 days after the expert's last answer** on your request.
- The rest of your request (parameters, name, verdict and analysis texts) is deleted **30 days** after the expert's last answer.
- If the expert asks you to retake your photos, the old photos are deleted after 7 days; the request waits for the new ones.
- After a "Potential" or "Borderline" verdict, the full review can be bought within 7 days, while the photos are still stored.
- The device mark is kept after your request is deleted: it holds no personal data and stops a second free review from the same iPhone.
- You can **delete all your data at any time** in Settings → Delete my data. This removes your requests, photos, parameters, results and your anonymous account from our servers. If you bought the full review, it will be lost after deletion. Purchase records stay with Apple and RevenueCat, and a record that a purchase was used stays on our server without your user ID. The device mark also stays, so a new free review from this iPhone is not possible.
- Results are linked to this device. If you delete the app or change devices, you may lose access to them. Deleting the app does not remove the key from the Keychain.

## Purchases
The full review is a one-time in-app purchase ("Full review"), available only after a "Potential" or "Borderline" verdict. Apple charges it; RevenueCat confirms the purchase to us under your anonymous ID.

## Refunds
Purchases are processed by Apple. To request a refund, use reportaproblem.apple.com.
